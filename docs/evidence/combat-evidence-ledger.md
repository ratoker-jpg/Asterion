## Evidence handling

The archive audit report and row-level transition CSV are the reproducible evidence index for this work. The 1,472 battle JSONL records are separated into capture errors and usable reports; campaign events and manifest factors are treated as harness metadata, not battle results or game rules. The 6,052 target switches are grouped by the recorded experiment block, and all held-out blocks are checked separately from `target_priority`. No Nemexia seed is recorded. Confirmed round-response rules, Aegis Destroyer Revival amount/timing, and tooltip-backed Shmel freezing are active in the shared resolver; exact target choice, extra-action cause/cadence, Revival/freezing RNG granularity, and Nemexia's exact within-side shuffle remain explicitly uncalibrated. Final production-mode playback covered all 1,363 clean cases × 3 Asterion seeds (4,089 battles), including the 12 support-order cases omitted by the prior runner: 25,732/25,732 phase-order checks and 102,531/102,531 defender-count checks passed. Winner totals are diagnostic only because Nemexia seed and exact catalog parity are unavailable. No points, rewards, ship stats, or balance values were changed.

The ledger does not infer mechanics from a single win, a single loss, or an untracked random outcome. Every report records the selected profile, technology mode, execution mode, target-selection status, and RNG provenance; legacy reports receive `unknown`/`non-replayable` markers instead of reconstructed numeric values.
