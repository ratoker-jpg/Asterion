## Evidence handling

The archive audit report and row-level transition CSV are the reproducible evidence index for this work. The 1,472 battle JSONL records are separated into capture errors and usable reports; campaign events and manifest factors are treated as harness metadata, not battle results or game rules. The 6,052 target switches are grouped by the recorded experiment block, and all held-out blocks are checked separately from `target_priority`. No Nemexia seed is recorded. Confirmed round-response rules are active in the shared resolver; exact target choice, extra-action cause/cadence, ability formulas, and within-side ordering remain explicitly uncalibrated. No points, rewards, ship stats, or balance values were changed.

The ledger does not infer mechanics from a single win, a single loss, or an untracked random outcome. Every report records the selected profile, technology mode, execution mode, target-selection status, and RNG provenance; legacy reports receive `unknown`/`non-replayable` markers instead of reconstructed numeric values.
